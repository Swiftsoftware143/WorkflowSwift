1. What this is

This is the mail client for the inbox that comes with your WorkflowSwift account.

It is where you read and answer the mail that arrives on your own addresses on your own domain. It is not a separate product with a separate password: it is the same account you already use in WorkflowSwift, so the addresses, the storage and the mailbox list are the same ones your account owns.

You open it from the Inbox screen of WorkflowSwift ("Open the mail client"), or straight at this address. Both show the same mail.

2. Signing in

Press Log In and use the email address and password of your WorkflowSwift account. There is no second password to remember and nothing extra to enable.

  • The same login covers every mailbox on the account — you choose which one to read after signing in.
  • If you have forgotten the password, reset it from the WorkflowSwift sign-in screen; the mail client uses the same password.

3. Setting up an inbox

Six short steps take you from a domain you already own to a mailbox that receives. Nothing here needs a technician.

  1. Add your domain. The domain you want to receive mail on. It stays yours — the records below are added wherever the domain already lives, and nothing is transferred to us.
  2. Choose how you send and how you receive. These are two separate answers: your own Mailgun account or any SMTP service to send, and Mailgun, SendGrid (or nowhere at all) to receive.
  3. Add the records your provider gives you. Every value comes with a Copy button, and the list is exactly what your provider issued — not a document someone typed by hand.
  4. Wait for the domain to say Active. Press the check again after the records have spread; the create-mailbox button stays disabled until the domain is Active.
  5. Create the addresses. Each one is a real mailbox with its own counts and its own signature.
  6. Turn on catch-all if you want one inbox to catch everything. Mail to any address at your domain you have not created still arrives, and it is shown under Everything else in the address filter.
The free month starts at your first mailbox, not at signup. Time spent waiting for DNS to spread costs you nothing.
Grey cloud and orange cloud. Every mail record stays DNS only (grey cloud) at Cloudflare, except the inbox record, which is set to Proxied (orange cloud). If a record is proxied when it should not be, the domain will not verify.

4. Choosing a mailbox

After signing in you get one card per address on the account, each showing the address and how many messages it holds. Nothing opens behind your back — you pick the address you want to read.

  • The count on each card is that address's own mail, not the whole account's.
  • To switch to another address later, press Mailboxes in the left rail.
  • An account with no mailboxes yet lands on the setup page instead.

5. The mail screen

Three parts: the rail on the left, the message list in the middle, the reader on the right.

The rail

  • Compose — start a new message. Drafts are kept under Drafts and can be continued or discarded.
  • Signature — set this address's own signature, or turn it off.
  • Folders — Inbox, Unread, Favorites, Drafts, Sent, Archive, Spam and Trash, each with its live count.
  • Folders you made — your own folders and subfolders, created from a folder's menu.
  • Labels — your labels, each with its colour. Press a label to filter the list to it.
  • Storage meter — how much of the allowance this account has used (see section 9).

The address filter

Above the list you can narrow it to one address on the account. When catch-all has caught something, an Everything else entry appears, so mail to an address you never created is still visible instead of disappearing.

Keeping the list current

Press Refresh when new mail is due. Folders and counts refresh with it.

6. Working with a message

Open a message and the reader offers:

ActionWhat it does
Reply / Reply allAnswers the sender, or everyone on the message. Your signature is added for you.
ForwardSends the message on to someone else.
☆ StarMarks the message; starred mail is reachable from Favorites.
ArchiveFiles it out of the Inbox into Archive.
Move to…Moves the message into any folder or subfolder you made.
DeleteMoves it to Trash — in Spam and Trash the same button says Delete forever.
LabelAttaches one of your labels to this message, or removes it again.
AssignHands the message to a member of the account, so someone owns the answer.
SummariseOffered when an AI summary is available for the message.
Turn into a ticketTurns the message into a support ticket in WorkflowSwift, so it is tracked like any other ticket.

7. Folder menu

The three-dot menu beside a folder in the rail acts on the whole folder:

  • Create subfolder — nests a folder inside this one. Names are stored lower-case.
  • Mark all as read / Mark all as unread.
  • Archive only: Move all to Inbox, or Delete all messages.
  • Spam and Trash only: Empty folder — it names the count first and cannot be undone.
  • Sort alphabetically — orders the folders.

8. Labels

Labels are your own coloured tags, attached to individual messages — a message can carry more than one, and a label never moves a message out of its folder.

  • Press + beside Labels in the rail to create one: a name and a colour.
  • Each label's three-dot menu offers Edit and Delete. Deleting a label does not touch a single message.
  • Press a label in the rail to filter the list to the messages carrying it.
  • Inside a message, the label control attaches a label to that message or removes it.

9. Storage and reminders

The meter in the rail shows how much of the allowance the account has used.

  • Incoming mail is never refused, and nothing is deleted to make room for new mail.
  • When the account reaches 50%, 75% and 99%, a banner appears in the client and one reminder email is sent to the owner of the account — one per rung, not one per day.
  • At 100%, new attachments and files pause until space is freed. Mail still arrives and is still readable.
Free space at any time by emptying Spam or Trash from their folder menu, or by deleting the messages you no longer need.

10. Signature

Each address has its own signature: name, title, company, phone and a short line. Press Signature in the rail to set it for the address you are reading, or to turn it off for that address.

Compose and Reply add the signature for you. The account-wide default is set by the operator on the Email Branding screen, so a new address starts with something sensible instead of nothing.

11. When something is not working

The domain never says Active

Almost always a record. Compare what your DNS provider holds against the values the app gave you (they have Copy buttons), and check that the mail records are grey cloud while only the inbox record is proxied. Press the check again once the change has spread.

Mail to an address is missing

If the address was never created, the message is only caught when catch-all is on — look under Everything else in the address filter. If catch-all is off, mail to an unknown address is refused by design.

I cannot create a mailbox

The create button stays disabled until the domain is Active. That is deliberate: a mailbox on a domain that cannot receive yet would silently lose mail.

I cannot send

Sending needs a sending provider on the setup page — your own Mailgun account or any SMTP service. Receiving can be set up without sending; mail still arrives and is readable.

I deleted something by mistake

Anything deleted from the Inbox goes to Trash first, where it can be recovered. Delete forever and Empty folder are the only actions that cannot be undone — both name what they will destroy before they do it.
