Roadmap

What we are building next, what is being built right now, and what has already shipped. This page changes as we ship — nothing here is a promise of a date.

Planned

On the list, not started.

  • Richer inboxFolders, starring, drafts and archive in the private email client.
  • Storage meterA clear usage meter with a warning before you run out.
  • More email providersSend and receive through another provider as well as Mailgun.

In progress

Being built right now.

  • Self-serve checkoutBuy a plan on the site instead of filing a request.
  • Workspace switcherSwitch, add, rename and delete workspaces without signing in again.

Shipped

Live in your console today.

  • Submit feedbackSend an idea, a bug or a complaint from inside the console.
  • Email brandingUpload your logo and set a signature once; it rides every message you send.
  • MCP accessRead this workspace from your own AI assistant with an API key — read-only by design.
  • Plan requestsAsk for a plan and follow the request on your dashboard.
  • Private email inboxIncoming mail beside ticketing, in the same console.
