What’s new

Every change below is live in your console. Newest first.

20 November 2026

  • Mail that reaches people who want it. A workflow notification now carries a working way out, and an opt-out is honoured — an address that opted out is reported as suppressed instead of looking sent.
  • The inbox opens without a second sign-in. Pressing the mail-client door on the Inbox card carries your session through.
  • Credit packs in the admin console. List, create, re-price and retire credit packs, with the same card on the customer's screen.
  • The MCP screen is documented. The guide now shows the endpoint, your key, the tools the server answers and the connect block to paste into your assistant.
  • The “running now” panel loads. The workspace dashboard call it needed is defined, so the panel paints instead of spinning.

19 November 2026

  • Honest request copy. The “a receipt is on its way” sentence only appears when the server actually queued the receipt.
  • Closing a plan request cannot downgrade you. A request closed after you moved higher leaves your plan alone.
  • Reset links are stored hashed. A password-reset token is kept as a digest, never as the value that was mailed.
  • Checkout refusals no longer lose the sale. When a payment provider refuses the session, the request reaches our team instead of vanishing.
  • Cross-app destinations work. FunnelSwift and IncentiveSwift destinations now resolve and parse.

18 November 2026

  • Quieter operator alerts. Test addresses on the brand domain no longer page the operator.
  • Your words reach the public pricing page. Anything you write about payment on your account appears where buyers read it.
